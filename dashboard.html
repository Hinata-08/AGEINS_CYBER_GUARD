Privacy Policy & Zero-Knowledge Protection

AGEINS Cyber Guard is committed to user data privacy and zero-knowledge security:

  • Client-Side PII Protection: Personally identifiable data (phone numbers, bank accounts) are automatically masked prior to processing.
  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: Passwords are protected using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with unique 16-byte random salts.
  • Organiser Security Controls: User login records are accessible strictly via authorized Organiser credentials.
  • Ephemeral File Handling: Uploaded media evidence is processed in RAM and automatically scrubbed after forensic analysis.

Cyber Safety & Fraud Prevention Guidelines

Follow these 5 golden rules to protect yourself against financial fraud and deepfake scams:

  1. Never Share OTPs or PINs: No bank official or government authority will ever ask for your UPI PIN or OTP.
  2. PIN is ONLY for Sending Money: You never need to enter a UPI PIN to RECEIVE money or claim cashbacks.
  3. Verify VPA Handles: Double-check recipient VPA names on your banking screen before confirming payments.
  4. Double-Check Deepfake Calls: Verify voice calls or video requests via an independent phone call before wiring money.
  5. Report Immediately to 1930: Report financial fraud within 24 hours to the National Cybercrime Helpline 1930.
